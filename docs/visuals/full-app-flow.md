# Full-app visual exploration

Historical concept record. The active frontend is the frontend Next.js app; [status](../status.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) and [scene contracts](../technical/scenes.md) describe the current implementation. The rejected separate Vite prototype has been removed.

## Direction selection

Worlds was selected on 2026-11-22 as the broad visual direction. Atlas remains an unselected comparison. [Open the preserved visual comparison](../../design-explorations/full-app-flow/comparison.html).

- **Worlds:** a cinematic museum atmosphere across discovery, stories and supporting account/creation screens. The hub remains multi-topic, with obvious question entry, search and continuation.
- **Atlas:** a brighter mineral/ultramarine geographic interpretation. This comparison was not an instruction to make geography compulsory.

The boards include desktop hub, mobile exploration/player and account/creation studies. Their catalogs, ratings, dates, geometry and progress numbers are illustrative. Generated imagery is not historical evidence, and the concepts do not establish working authentication, generation or community services. Source prompt records remain alongside the boards.

## Relationship to the implementation

The user selected a direction, not a fixed raster contract. Later decisions establish four semantic themes, native 3D, real-image evidence studies, complete reading, exact bookmarks and an agent-authorable shared scene system. These supersede literal board colors, catalog counts and spatial arrangements. The latest user screenshots and feedback outrank these earlier references.

Four three-chapter storyboards and 18 teaching presets now demonstrate the shared renderer. Current image-led layouts place substantial art beside reading, keep selected details at the artwork, switch between large comparison images and protect initial hotspot reachability. Maps and objects have their own measured/compact presentation. Their rendered acceptance remains pending.
